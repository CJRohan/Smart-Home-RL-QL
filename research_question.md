# Research question

## Plain-English version

For an isolated home with solar panels, a diesel generator, and a battery — but no electricity grid — how should we decide when to run household appliances and when to run the generator?

The schedule should use as much solar energy as possible, avoid unnecessary diesel-generator use, and still meet household tasks and comfort preferences.

## Formal version

Over a planning horizon, find a schedule of appliance operation and generator operation that:

- minimises diesel-generator operation and wasted renewable energy;
- meets appliance/task requirements and deadlines where possible;
- keeps the battery from being depleted; and
- supports comfort preferences such as AC/heater use.

## System boundary

The home is in an island/isolated setting:

- energy sources: solar panel and diesel generator;
- energy storage: home battery;
- electricity users: laundry, dishwasher, oven, refrigerator, TV/PC, AC/heater and scooter charging;
- no external electric grid.

The energy route is fixed: solar and generator feed the home battery; appliances use energy from the battery.

## First proposed scope for discussion

Start with one representative day. A later version can extend the same model to several days and weekly deadlines, for example scooter charging every five days or dishwasher operation every two days.
